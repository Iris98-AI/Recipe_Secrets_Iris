Our Story

Recipe Secrets began as a small family kitchen passing down recipes through generations. Today, we've grown into a beloved dining room in Singapore, but the heart of what we do hasn't changed: honest ingredients, patient technique, and dishes made to be shared.

Every recipe on our menu carries a memory — a grandmother's broth, a street-side stall, a quiet Sunday lunch — reimagined for the modern table.

Meet the Chef

Chef Amara Lim

Head Chef Amara Lim trained in both traditional Cantonese kitchens and modern European restaurants before returning to Singapore to open Recipe Secrets. Her cooking bridges two worlds — bold, comforting flavours plated with contemporary precision.

"I want every guest to taste a memory, even if it's not their own," she says. Amara leads the kitchen with a hands-on, seasonal approach, reworking the menu as new ingredients come into harvest.

Philosophy

Our Philosophy & Ingredients

We believe great food starts long before it reaches the pass. We work with local farmers and fishmongers, favour whole ingredients over shortcuts, and change our menu with the seasons. Nothing is wasted, everything is intentional — from the broth simmered overnight to the herbs grown on our rooftop.
